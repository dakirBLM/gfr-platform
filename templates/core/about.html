About

An integrated global academic ecosystem.

The Global Forum for Researchers (GFR) brings together researchers, scientists, and postgraduate students from around the world. It is not merely a publishing site — it is an integrated digital ecosystem that supports research, collaboration, peer-reviewed publishing, and the dissemination of knowledge that contributes to sustainable societal impact.

Vision

To become a leading global platform that advances scientific research and innovation while building an integrated international academic community that produces impactful knowledge contributing to societal development.

Mission

To empower researchers by providing access to scientific knowledge, enabling peer-reviewed publishing, supporting collaborative projects, and strengthening cooperation between researchers, institutions, and economic sectors.

Core values

  • Academic integrity
  • Objectivity and quality assurance
  • Transparency
  • International scientific collaboration
  • Innovation
  • Community service

Governance

GFR operates through six bodies: a Board of Directors, a Scientific Council, a Journal Peer Review Committee, an Editorial Board, a Research Projects Committee, and a Conferences & Workshops Committee.
