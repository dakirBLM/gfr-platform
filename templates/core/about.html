About GFR

An Integrated Global
Academic Ecosystem.

{# ---------- Intro ---------- #}

Not merely a publishing site — a place where research, people, and institutions meet.

The Global Forum for Researchers brings together researchers, scientists, and postgraduate students from around the world. It is an integrated digital ecosystem that supports research, collaboration, peer-reviewed publishing, and the dissemination of knowledge that contributes to sustainable societal impact.

{# ---------- Vision / Mission ---------- #}

Vision

To become a leading global platform that advances scientific research and innovation while building an integrated international academic community that produces impactful knowledge contributing to societal development.

Mission

To empower researchers by providing access to scientific knowledge, enabling peer-reviewed publishing, supporting collaborative projects, and strengthening cooperation between researchers, institutions, and economic sectors.

{# ---------- Core values ---------- #}

What We Stand For

Six principles that shape every review, project, and publication on GFR.

Academic integrity
Objectivity and quality assurance
Transparency
International collaboration
Innovation
Community service
{# ---------- Governance ---------- #}

Governance

Six bodies, one standard.

GFR operates through a Board of Directors, a Scientific Council, a Journal Peer Review Committee, an Editorial Board, a Research Projects Committee, and a Conferences & Workshops Committee.
